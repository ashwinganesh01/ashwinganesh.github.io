Explore the Galaxy

About the Milky Way

The Milky Way is the galaxy that contains our Solar System. It is made up of billions of stars, planets, moons, dust, and gas spread across space.

Scientists believe the Milky Way is more than 13 billion years old. Our Solar System sits inside one of its spiral arms, far away from the galaxy's centre.

Even travelling at incredible speeds, crossing the Milky Way would take thousands of years because of its massive size.

Explore the Planets

Earth

Earth is the only known planet that supports life. It has oceans, forests, mountains, and an atmosphere filled with oxygen.

Learn More

Mars

Mars is often called the Red Planet because of its reddish surface. Scientists continue to study whether humans may one day live there.

Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System. It is famous for the Great Red Spot, a giant storm that has lasted for many years.

Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and also the smallest planet in our Solar System. Temperatures there can be extremely hot or freezing cold.

Venus

Venus is the hottest planet in the Solar System. Thick clouds trap heat and create very high temperatures.

Explore Venus

Saturn

Saturn is known for its beautiful icy rings. It is one of the largest gas giants orbiting the Sun.

Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant with a blue appearance caused by methane gas. Unlike most planets, it rotates on its side.

Neptune

Neptune is the farthest known planet from the Sun. It has freezing temperatures and some of the strongest winds in space.

Explore Neptune

Planet Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the home of all known life. Around 71% of Earth's surface is covered with water.

  • Distance from the Sun: 149.6 million km
  • Length of a day: 24 hours
  • Number of moons: 1
  • Atmosphere contains oxygen and nitrogen
